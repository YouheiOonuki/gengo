"After birthday" is the age once the birthday in that year has passed; "Before birthday" is the age until then. Years with an era change list both eras (S64/H1 = Shōwa 64 / Heisei 1).

Japanese eras since 1868

EraReadingBeganEndedYears

"Began" is the day the era changed, not 1 January. Meiji 1 was counted back to the first day of the lunisolar year (25 January 1868). Sources are listed in the guide.

Reading dates on Japanese documents

  • 年 月 日 mean year, month and day: 令和8年9月24日 is Reiwa 8, September 24 = 24 September 2026.
  • 元年 (gannen) means year 1 of an era: 令和元年 = Reiwa 1 = 2019.
  • Forms often use one letter: R Reiwa, H Heisei, S Shōwa, T Taishō, M Meiji. H1.1.8 is 8 January 1989.
  • To convert quickly: Reiwa + 2018, Heisei + 1988, Shōwa + 1925 = Western year.
