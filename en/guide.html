Japanese era years and dates on documents

Add 2018 to a Reiwa year, 1988 to Heisei and 1925 to Shōwa to get the Western year. Dates are written year, month, day: 令和8年9月24日 is 24 September 2026, and 元年 (gannen) means year 1.

  • 年 (nen) is year, 月 (gatsu) is month, 日 (nichi) is day. 生年月日 means date of birth; 西暦 is the Western year; 和暦 is the Japanese era calendar.
  • Short forms use the era's first letter: H1.1.8 is 8 January 1989.
  • Formal documents may use kanji numerals: 昭和六十年三月五日 is Shōwa 60, March 5. The converter reads these.
  • Meiji dates before 1 January 1873 were in the lunisolar calendar, so only the year is converted.

Frequently asked questions

What year is Showa 60?
1985. Add 1925 to any Shōwa year; add 1988 to Heisei and 2018 to Reiwa.
When did Reiwa start, and why does 1989 have Showa 64 and Heisei 1?
Reiwa began on 1 May 2019. Eras change on the day set by law, so Shōwa 64 covers only 1–7 January 1989.
How do I write my date of birth on a Japanese form?
Circle the era (昭和・平成・令和), then write year, month and day. Born 10 May 1990: circle 平成, write 2 年 5 月 10 日.
How is age counted in Japan?
Like most countries: one year older on each birthday. Legally it happens at the end of the day before; someone born on 29 February turns older at the end of 28 February in other years.
How does the Japanese school year work?
It runs from April to March. Children born 2 April to 1 April the next year share a grade and start elementary school the April after turning 6.
