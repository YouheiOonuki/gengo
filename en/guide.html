Japanese era years: guide and FAQ

How to convert them, what gannen means, era change days, age and the school year in Japan

How to use the converter

  1. Japanese era ⇄ Western year: type "Showa 60", "H31.4.30", "令和元年五月一日", "1989" or "1985-03-05". A Western year on its own lists every Japanese era year it contains (two in a year when the era changed).
  2. Age, zodiac and school year: enter a date of birth in either calendar to get the age on the reference date (today by default), the traditional count, the zodiac sign, the Japanese school grade, and the years of entering and graduating each school (useful for a Japanese CV, 履歴書).
  3. Age chart: choose a year and press Print. One A4 page lists every birth year with its Japanese era, zodiac and age before and after the birthday.

In a year when the era changed, the old era runs until the day before the change and the new one starts on the day itself: 7 January 1989 is Shōwa 64 and 8 January 1989 is Heisei 1.

Reading dates on Japanese documents

  • Dates are written year, month, day: 令和8年9月24日 = Reiwa 8, month 9, day 24 = 24 September 2026. 年 (nen) is year, 月 (gatsu) is month, 日 (nichi) is day.
  • 元年 (gannen) is year 1 of an era.
  • Short forms use the first letter of the era: R8.9.24, H1.1.8, S60.3.5.
  • Kanji numerals may appear in formal documents: 昭和六十年三月五日 is Shōwa 60, March 5. The converter reads these too.
  • 生年月日 (seinengappi) means date of birth; 西暦 (seireki) means the Western year; 和暦 (wareki) means the Japanese era calendar.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert a Japanese era year to a Western year?
Add a fixed number: Reiwa + 2018, Heisei + 1988, Shōwa + 1925, Taishō + 1911, Meiji + 1867. Shōwa 60 is 1985, Heisei 2 is 1990 and Reiwa 8 is 2026.
What year is Reiwa 1, and when did Reiwa start?
Reiwa 1 is 2019. The Reiwa era began on 1 May 2019; 1 January to 30 April 2019 was Heisei 31. The cabinet order took effect "the day after 30 April Heisei 31" (Cabinet Order No. 143 of 2019, in Japanese).
What does 元年 (gannen) mean?
It means the first year of an era, the same as year 1. 令和元年 (Reiwa gannen) is 2019 and 平成元年 (Heisei gannen) is 1989.
Why does 1989 have both Shōwa 64 and Heisei 1?
The era changes on the day set by law, not on 1 January. Shōwa 64 lasted only from 1 to 7 January 1989, and Heisei 1 began on 8 January 1989 (the cabinet order was promulgated on 7 January and took effect the next day; Cabinet Order No. 1 of 1989).
How do I write my date of birth on a Japanese form?
Many forms print the era names (昭和・平成・令和) for you to circle, followed by 年 (year), 月 (month) and 日 (day). Someone born on 10 May 1990 circles 平成 and writes 2 年 5 月 10 日. If the form allows it, you can also write the Western year (西暦).
How is age counted in Japan?
Everyday and legal age is 満年齢 (man-nenrei), counted like in most countries: 0 at birth, one year older on each birthday. Legally, age is counted from the day of birth (Act on Counting Age) and a year ends on the day before the corresponding date (Civil Code Art. 143), so you become a year older at the end of the day before your birthday. The traditional count, 数え年 (kazoedoshi), starts at 1 and adds a year every 1 January; it is still used for some customs.
When does someone born on 29 February become a year older?
Under Japanese law, at the end of 28 February in non-leap years, so they are the new age on 1 March. This follows the Civil Code rule that a period with no corresponding day ends on the last day of that month.
How does the school year work in Japan?
The school year runs from 1 April to 31 March (Enforcement Regulations of the School Education Act Art. 59). Children start elementary school at the first school year that begins on or after the day after they reach 6 (School Education Act Art. 17). So children born between 2 April and 1 April of the next year are in the same grade. Those born 1 January–1 April are called 早生まれ (hayaumare) and are the youngest in their grade.
When will the era change again?
Under the Era Name Act, the era is set by cabinet order and changes only when a new emperor succeeds to the throne. No date is fixed in advance.
Is my date of birth stored?
No. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is stored or sent.
