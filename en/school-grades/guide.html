The April 2 cutoff, school names and how long each school lasts

  4. Press Print. The chart fits one A4 or Letter page.

Why the cutoff is 2 April

Children start elementary school at the first school year that begins on or after the day after they turn six (School Education Act, Article 17). The school year runs from 1 April to 31 March.

Japanese law counts a new year of age as reached at the end of the day before the birthday. A child born on 1 April turns six on 31 March and starts school the next day. A child born on 2 April turns six on 1 April, after the year has begun, and waits until the following April.

Japanese school names

Japan uses 6 years of elementary school, 3 of junior high and 3 of high school. The first 9 years are compulsory.

Frequently asked questions

Which grade is a child born in 2019 in Japan?
In the 2026–27 school year, children born 2 April 2019 to 1 April 2020 are in elementary grade 1. Those born 1 January to 1 April 2019 are in grade 2.
Why is a child born on April 1 in the grade above April 2?
Under Japanese law a person turns a year older at the end of the day before the birthday. A child born on 1 April turns six on 31 March, just before the school year starts.
What does hayaumare mean?
Hayaumare ("early born") means born between 1 January and 1 April. These children join the grade of those born from 2 April of the previous year, so they are the youngest in their class.
When does the school year start and end in Japan?
Elementary, junior high and high schools run from 1 April to 31 March. Each university sets its own start and end dates.
